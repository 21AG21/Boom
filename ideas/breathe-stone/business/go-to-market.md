# Breathe Stone — Go-to-Market

## Positioning (one line)
**"The $199 breathing coach, reinvented as a $34 stone you keep in your
pocket — no app, no charging drama, no screen."**

Moonbird proved people will pay for a handheld tactile breathing pacer.
Breathe Stone takes that proven demand down-market and into everyday carry.

### Positioning map

| | Moonbird | Breathing apps (Calm/Headspace) | **Breathe Stone** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | ~$199 | $70/yr | **$34 (or $14 kinetic)** |
| Interface | whole device expands in hand | screen | **a thumb pad on a pebble** |
| Screen/app | optional app, biofeedback | required | **none, ever** |
| Carry | a gadget in a case | your phone | **an object in your pocket** |
| Bulk/institutional | no (price) | no (per-seat) | **yes (kinetic Calm Kit)** |

Don't fight Moonbird on biofeedback features — win on price, pocketability,
and the fact that at rest it's just a nice stone.

## Messaging pillars
1. **Anti-app.** "The one wellness tool that isn't another screen." Rides the
   2026 screen-fatigue / dopamine-detox backlash.
2. **It disappears into your life.** Not a medical gadget — a worry stone that
   happens to breathe. Use it in a meeting, on a plane, in the dark.
3. **Works for people apps can't reach.** Kids, elderly, non-verbal, mid-panic.
4. **Silent by design.** (The engineering story — silent-hold firmware — is
   also a trust signal that this is made by people who care.)

Avoid medical claims. Market as comfort/wellness ("supports calm, paced
breathing"), never as treatment for a condition — that keeps you clear of
FDA device regulation and false-advertising risk.

## Phased plan

### Phase 0 — Validate & protect (weeks 1–4, ~$165)
- Build the prototype from `hardware/` + `firmware/`; run the **ten-hands
  test** (see `../../one-month-ship-plan.md`).
- File the **provisional patent** (draft in `../patent/`), ~$65.
- Cut one **8-second video**: anxious person, thumb on the stone, breathing
  visibly slows. This asset carries the whole launch.

### Phase 1 — Crowdfund the tooling (months 1–3)
- Kickstarter/Indiegogo pre-sale, target **1,000–2,000 units** to fund
  injection tooling before spending on at-risk inventory.
- Lead creative: the Phase-0 video + the Moonbird price contrast ("$199 →
  $34").
- Seed 50 units to anxiety/ADHD/therapist creators and sensory-toy reviewers
  pre-launch for day-one social proof.

### Phase 2 — DTC scale (months 3–9)
- Tooled electronic version live on Shopify + Amazon.
- Organic short-video engine (the product films itself); light paid retarget.
- PR angle: **"the anti-app anxiety tool"** — pitch wellness and tech-culture
  press on the screen-fatigue narrative.
- Begin retail wholesale into gift/pharmacy/museum counters.

### Phase 3 — Open the moat: B2B + collectibles (months 9–18)
- Launch the **kinetic Calm Kit** for institutions: airlines (fear-of-flying
  kits), ER/dental waiting rooms, schools, therapists. This is the channel
  Moonbird's price locks it out of, and it's recurring.
- Start the **restock loop**: seasonal silicone dome "skins," a scented line,
  a weighted night edition, a kids edition — the reason to buy the next one.
- Use B2B contracts + brand + IP + the collectible loop as the defense
  against the commoditization that killed the fidget spinner.

## Channel economics snapshot
- **DTC:** ~64% gross margin; CAC is the risk → lean on organic video.
- **Wholesale:** thinner (~29%) but buys shelf presence and credibility.
- **B2B/institutional:** kinetic version, volume, low CAC (sales
  conversations not ads), reorders — the healthiest long-term leg.

## Risks & mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **Moonbird / IP** — close incumbent, possible patents | Compete on price+form+B2B, not features; file provisional; get a pro search before non-provisional |
| **Commoditization** (the spinner's fate) | Brand, B2B contracts, collectible restock, design IP |
| **Efficacy skepticism / regulatory** | No medical claims; position as comfort/wellness; paced breathing is well-supported but market it as support, not treatment |
| **Actuator noise** | Silent-hold firmware; MG90S; or the kinetic (mechanical) version |
| **Dome durability** (hand oils, thousands of cycles) | Material spec + cycle-test in Phase 1; captured-lip design already isolates the flexing zone |
| **CAC on DTC** | The product is inherently video-shareable; B2B sidesteps ad spend entirely |

waiting room in the country."
