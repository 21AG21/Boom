# Seed Fidget 🌱 — Deep-Dive One-Pager

> *Fidget with it for a month. When you're bored, plant it — it grows
> wildflowers.*

Flagship #3 from the [Idea Vault](../README.md). A fully biodegradable fidget
molded from a seed-embedded composite. Satisfying and durable enough to carry
for weeks; when you're done, plant it in soil, water it, and it sprouts. The
fidget's **ending** is the shareable moment, not just its use.

## Materials & mechanism (where the real R&D is)
The hard part is a **durable-in-use, compostable-and-germinating-later**
object — an inherent tension the research pass flagged:
- **PLA bioplastic** is rigid and durable but only breaks down in *industrial*
  compost (~55–60 °C) — not true "plant it in your yard."
- **PHA / starch blends** are home- and marine-compostable but softer and
  moisture-sensitive.
- **Seed viability** is the killer constraint: seeds must survive
  manufacturing temperatures.

**Design answers:**
1. **Molded paper-pulp / seed-paper composite** body (like premium egg-carton
   pulp), home-compostable, with seeds **embedded in a cool surface layer
   after** the hot forming step so they stay viable.
2. **Bearing-free mechanics** so the *whole* object is compostable — a
   flex-click coin, a snap-cube, or a pulp spinner on a wooden or
   compostable-ceramic pivot (never a steel bearing).
3. A **thin moisture barrier** (e.g., a wax or dissolvable coating) that
   survives pocket use and washes away on planting.
4. A printed **"best planted by" date** — seeds have shelf life.

Prototype the compression **molds** on this repo's 3D-print pipeline; iterate
geometry parametrically before committing to material.

## Patent claim skeleton
- **Independent:** *A manipulable tactile fidget object comprising a
  seed-bearing compostable matrix and a bearing-free actuation feature,
  configured to withstand a plurality of manipulation cycles during a service
  life and thereafter to biodegrade and germinate the borne seeds when planted
  and watered.*
- **Dependent:** seeds disposed in a **post-formed surface layer** to preserve
  viability through molding; a moisture barrier that degrades on planting;
  specific bearing-free mechanisms (flex-click coin, snap-cube, pulp spinner
  on a compostable pivot); an embossed brand/logo region; a home-compostable
  vs. industrial-compostable material selection.
- Novelty is surviving weeks of high-touch mechanical use **and then**
  reliably germinating — an unusual combined materials + mechanism
  requirement.

## Go-to-market

2. **Promotional-products channel (volume)** — pitch the swag/agency world:
   logo-embossed plantable fidgets for events and corporate gifting.
3. **Restock loop** — monthly seed varieties (poppies, then basil, then a
   pollinator mix) to collect.

## Risks & mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **Durability vs. compostability** trade-off | Pulp body + bearing-free mechanics + moisture coating; cycle-test early |
| **Seed viability** through manufacturing | Embed seeds in a cool post-forming surface layer |
| **Invasive-species / regulatory** risk | Use only **non-invasive, region-appropriate** wildflower or culinary-herb seed; label by region; this is a real compliance item, not a footnote |
| **Seed shelf life** | "Best planted by" date; moisture-sealed packaging |
| Knockoffs | Brand + the corporate-channel relationships + IP |

## First build (this week)
Mold a plain seed-pulp coin, carry it for two weeks, then plant it. If it
**survives the pocket** *and* **sprouts**, the hard part is solved — everything
after is form and branding.
