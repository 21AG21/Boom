01

The bar

The spinner won on tactility and timing, not technology. Every idea here is scored on the five traits that made it unstoppable — and read through three lenses.

CheapBill of materials under ~$5; retails in the $15–40 impulse range.
InstantUnderstood in three seconds. No manual, no app required.
TactileRewards the hand — you don't want to stop touching it.
ShareableOne eight-second video does the selling.
RestockA reason to buy the next one — collectible, consumable, modular.

Three lenses

  • Money
    How it actually earns — the model, not just "sell it."
  • Patent
    The defensible, filable angle — form, mechanism, or method.
  • Society
    Who it genuinely helps, beyond the buyer.

The flagship three

Build these first. Deliberately diverse — one wellness, one behavioral, one climate — so the portfolio never rides on a single trend.

Flagship 01 · wellness
Fidget score●●●●●

Breathe Stone

"A worry stone that breathes with you — so you breathe with it."

A palm stone with a domed pad that slowly swells and falls on a 4-7-8 cadence. Rest your thumb on it and, without thinking, your breath entrains to the rise and fall. No screen. No notification. Near-silent.

Why nowThe only anxiety tools today are apps that add another screen to the problem. A calm object with zero UI is the contrarian — and correct — bet for a burnt-out, screen-fatigued 2026.

  • Money$29 impulse — but the real business is B2B: airline fear-of-flying kits, ER and dental waiting rooms, therapist bulk orders that reorder.
  • PatentA tactile-only respiratory pacer at a power budget low enough to run kinetic. Existing breathing trainers are visual or audible — never touch-only.
  • SocietyPaced breathing for the people an app can't reach: kids, dementia patients, non-verbal users, anyone mid-panic in public.
Flagship 02 · behavioral
Fidget score●●●●○

Focus Familiar

"Feed it your phone. It glows while you focus — and wilts the second you cave."

A desk creature with a slot in its belly. Slide your phone in and it wakes up and glows; pull it out early and it visibly wilts. Forest, the focus app, turned into a physical companion you'd feel guilty abandoning.

Why nowIt marries the dopamine-detox anxiety to the blind-box collectible mania (Labubu, Smiskis). Parents want to buy it; the collectible loop makes kids want it.

  • Money$35 base, but the engine is $9–12 blind-box creatures and rares — razor-and-blades fused to a socially-approved behavior.
  • PatentA focus companion whose expressive state is driven by the continuous presence — or early removal — of a stowed phone. The penalty animation is the defensible core.
  • SocietyA non-punitive way for families to cut screen time — it makes phone-down the fun choice, not the nagged one.
Flagship 03 · climate
Fidget score●●●●○

Seed Fidget

"Fidget with it for a month. When you're bored, plant it — it grows wildflowers."

A fully biodegradable fidget molded from a seed-embedded composite. Satisfying and durable enough to carry for weeks; when you're done, plant it and it sprouts. The ending is the shareable moment, not just the use.

Why nowPlantable goods already sell, but nobody has made a fidget — the ultimate throwaway-plastic object — out of one. The plant-and-timelapse is a video with a built-in beginning, middle, and end.

  • Money$12 seasonal impulse; the volume play is wedding, conference, and corporate favors — swag that leaves flowers instead of landfill.
  • PatentA durable-yet-compostable seed-matrix composite that survives weeks of high-touch use and then reliably germinates — an unusual materials requirement.
  • SocietyTurns the archetypal disposable gadget net-positive: zero landfill, and every one plants pollinator habitat.

Design principle

Every Tier B winner removes electronics rather than adding them. The differentiation isn't the sensor — it's the refusal of a screen. Screen fatigue is the whole tailwind.

05

Money plays & bigger swings

Less fidget-pure, more revenue and reach. Two of these ride the 3D-print pipeline already sitting in this repo.

C1SaaS · repo leverage

Fidgetsmith

Snap any object — your pet, your logo — and get a manufacturable, balanced fidget STL in 30 seconds. Freemium download, print-and-ship marketplace cut, creator subscriptions.

↳ extends hardware/generate_stls.py into a product

C2Subscription · digital

Blind-Print

A sealed mystery STL each week — you don't know which of the set you got until it prints. Digital scarcity, physical payoff, near-zero cost of goods. $9/mo, artist revenue-share.

C3Patent-heavy

Proof-of-Hand

A playful physical token that emits a time-based code when twisted in sequence — proving a real human did a real action. Bots can't twist a fidget. Events, ticketing, anti-scalper, classrooms.

D2Society-first · B2B

Calm Kit

Breathe Stones and Silent Ones licensed in bulk to ERs, dentists, airplanes, DMV lines — places humans are anxious and bored on purpose. Recurring B2B that measurably lowers waiting-room stress.

E1Moonshot · materials

Radiative-Cooling Carry

Passive daytime radiative-cooling film — cools below ambient with zero energy — productized for tents, strollers, dog houses, lunch bags. Patent the consumer form factors and lamination.

E2Moonshot · thermal

Cold Coaster

A coaster charged in the freezer that holds a drink cold for 2+ hours via phase-change material. Patent the recharge-in-freezer enclosure and thermal path.
